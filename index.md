## What We Collect
We keep it minimal — here's exactly what we gather:

### Account Data (Email Address)
When you create an account, we collect your email address (including a relay email address if you use Apple Sign In). This is used for account authentication and essential communications about your account.

### Sign in with Apple or Google
If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the minimum information necessary for authentication — typically your email address and a unique identifier. We do not access your contacts, photos, or any other personal information from these services.

---

## Learning Progress & Data Storage
How your learning data is stored depends on your account tier:

### Freemium (Free)
Your vocabulary lists, saved words, and learning progress are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data, and it cannot be synced or recovered if you change devices or uninstall the app.

### Premium (One-Time Purchase)
Like Freemium, all your learning data is stored locally on your device. Premium unlocks all app features but does not include cloud storage. Your data remains private and on-device.

### Pro (Subscription)
Pro subscribers get secure cloud storage and synchronization via Firebase. This includes your saved words, review history, and progress statistics — enabling synchronization across devices. Cloud data is protected with industry-standard security measures (including encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the provider).

**Summary**
- Freemium & Premium: Local storage only (no cloud learning-data collection)
- Pro: Local storage + cloud sync via Firebase

## Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Diagnostics)
Vocabin uses **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand app usage and improve stability.

We **do not show ads**, we **do not perform ad attribution**, and we **do not sell personal data**.

### Firebase Analytics
Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with the app (for example, which features are used and general app performance). Depending on your device and configuration, this may involve collecting app-instance identifiers and device/app information such as:
- app version
- device model
- OS version
- language/region
- approximate location derived from IP address

### Firebase Crashlytics
Firebase Crashlytics helps us diagnose crashes and errors. Crash reports may include information such as:
- device state at the time of the crash
- device model
- OS version
- app version
- crash timestamps
- identifiers needed to deliver crash reporting

### Purpose
We use these tools to:
- Improve your learning experience
- Identify and fix bugs quickly
- Ensure the app runs smoothly across devices

### Control / Consent
Where consent is required by law for analytics identifiers or similar technologies, we will request it and you can withdraw your choice at any time (for example, via in-app settings if available, or by contacting us).

Crash reporting may be processed under our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the app, subject to your rights under applicable law.

We collect data for the following purposes:
- To create and manage your Vocabin account
- To sync your learning progress across devices (**Pro only**)

- To improve Vocabin based on how it's being used (Firebase Analytics)
- To diagnose crashes and maintain app security and stability (Firebase Crashlytics)
- To provide customer support when you need it

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds (as applicable):

## How We Protect Your Data
Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. For Pro users, learning progress stored in Firebase is protected with robust security measures (including encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the provider) and access controls.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction of your data.

---

## Where Your Data Lives
For Freemium and Premium users, your learning data lives exclusively on your device.

For Pro users, your data may be processed and stored on Firebase servers. Depending on your location and the Firebase configuration, this may involve international data transfers. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (SCCs) or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

---

## How Long We Keep Your Data
We follow data minimization and retain data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy.

### Account Information (Email Address)
We retain your account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your account information unless we must keep certain data to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes (typically no longer than 30 days, unless legally required).

### For Pro Subscribers (Cloud Learning Data)
Your cloud learning data is retained while Pro is active. If you cancel Pro or downgrade, we will delete your cloud learning data within 30 days after the subscription ends (unless you reactivate Pro during that period).

### Firebase Analytics
We retain Firebase Analytics data for up to 14 months (or a shorter period if configured) to understand usage trends and improve the app.

### Firebase Crashlytics
We retain Crashlytics crash reports for a limited period appropriate to debugging and security (typically up to 90 days).

### Local-Only Data (Freemium & Premium)
For Freemium and Premium users, we do not receive your learning content because it remains on your device. You control retention by keeping or deleting the app's local data (for example, by deleting lists in-app or uninstalling the app).

---

## Account Deletion
You can delete your account in the app. When you do, we remove personal data we control from our systems, including Pro cloud learning data (if any).

**Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription** — you must cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings to stop billing.
